Questions from people who run a Legal team.

Does the AI approve contracts on its own?

No. The AI reviews against your playbook and proposes wording. A person always decides, and a failing clause blocks approval until a lawyer records the reason.

Do we have to migrate documents?

No. legalVector runs on the Alfresco or Doxis you already use. The repository stays the system of record.

Do the changes come out in Word?

Yes. Accepted fixes are genuine Word tracked changes, saved as a new version of the same file.

What doesn’t it do yet?

E-signature and Teams notifications are not included yet, and request intake is in beta. We’ll tell you plainly in the demo.
