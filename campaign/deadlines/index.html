Questions Legal teams ask.

Where are the dates stored?
As fields on the document in your repository: Alfresco properties or Doxis descriptors. Not in a separate database.
Does legalVector send reminders?
Legal sees what falls due in the next 30 and 60 days and what has passed, and can export the dates to a calendar as .ics. Ask us in the walkthrough how your team would follow them.
Do we have to migrate contracts?
No. legalVector runs on Alfresco Content Services or Doxis 4, and documents stay where they are.
Who reads the dates from the contract?
Texter Agent extracts them from the server side; Legal sees and owns the result.
Which languages?
English, Spanish, European Portuguese, French and Arabic.
