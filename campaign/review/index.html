Questions Legal teams ask.

Does the AI approve contracts?
No. It reviews against your playbook and proposes wording. Lawyers decide, and approval stays blocked while a must-fix finding is open unless a lawyer records a reason.
Is this legal advice?
No. legalVector assists your lawyers; it does not replace professional legal judgement.
Are the redlines real Word tracked changes?
Yes. Accepted fixes are saved as genuine tracked changes in a new version of the same file in your repository.
Whose playbook is used?
Yours: your organisation’s rules, with country overlays where they apply.
Does it replace Doxis Legal or our repository?
No. It runs on Alfresco Content Services or Doxis 4 and complements Doxis Legal.
Is there e-signature?
Not today. legalVector covers review, redlines and approval; signing stays where it is.
